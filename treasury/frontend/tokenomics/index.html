SHIT Price vs Floor vs NAV

Market price of SHIT compared to the Floor Price (intrinsic backing from treasury) and NAV per SHIT (total treasury value per token). Price above NAV = protocol is healthy.

SHIT Supply Growth

Total SHIT token supply and staked SHIT (stSHIT) supply over time. Supply grows from supplemental emissions; staking ratio increases as more holders stake.

Staking Ratio & APY

Percentage of SHIT that are staked (left axis) and the annualized staking APY yield (right axis). APY is driven by supplemental emissions which scale with price premium to NAV.

Staking Ratio & APY (Inverse Relationship)

Staking ratio (left axis) and APY (right axis) over time. As more holders stake, supplemental emissions are spread across more stSHIT, reducing per-token APY.

Staking Index (Rebase Accumulation)

Growth of 1 stSHIT over time via rebase rewards. A staking index of 2.0 means 1 stSHIT has grown to 2 SHIT worth of value through emissions.

Staked Supply Growth

stSHIT supply (staked tokens) compared to total SHIT supply. The gap between the lines shows the unstaked (floating) supply.

Circuit Breaker Status

Tracks how many consecutive epochs the market price has been near or below the floor price. If the count reaches the threshold (default 21), supplemental emissions are halted to prevent a death spiral.

Price vs Floor vs NAV (Per SHIT)

Same data as the Overview tab: Market Price, Floor Price (RFV per SHIT), and NAV per SHIT. Floor Price is the intrinsic backing value; NAV is the full treasury value per token.

Treasury Composition

Stacked breakdown of treasury assets over time: Floor Reserve, Band Reserve, Protocol Owned Liquidity, Yield Capital, and Backing Reserves. All grow from protocol fee revenue.

Floor Reserve & Redeemable Supply

Floor Reserve (treasury assets backing the floor price, left axis) and Redeemable Supply (SHIT eligible for redemption, right axis). Floor reserve should grow faster than redeemable supply for a healthy floor.

Market Price vs Floor Price (Premium/Discount)

Market price compared to the floor price. When market price is above floor, SHIT trades at a premium. When below, it trades at a discount to intrinsic value.

Price vs NAV (Premium Seller Zone)

Market Price, Floor Price, NAV per SHIT, and 2x NAV threshold. When price exceeds 2x NAV, the Premium Seller activates and mints SHIT (in small clips) to capture the premium and pull price back toward NAV.

SHIT Burned (Inverse Bonds)

Cumulative SHIT burned through inverse bonds. Holders burn SHIT in exchange for treasury assets, reducing supply. Burn rate increases when price is weak (near or below floor).

SHIT Sold (Premium Seller)

Cumulative SHIT minted and sold by the Premium Seller when price exceeds 2x NAV. This dilutes supply to pull price back down, capturing value for the treasury.
