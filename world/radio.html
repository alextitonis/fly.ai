Translation — live decoded reading

Captions appear once you've tuned in.

Every buzz above is real: the amplitude comes straight from the actual 166,700-neuron MaleCNS connectome, downloaded once (58 MB, the same files as the Simulation) and stepped live on your device — specifically each cast member's own wing-motor-neuron spikes above rest (the "song" flytalk.py measures). Nothing is pre-rendered or looped. The ~190 Hz sine tone that envelope rides on, and the show names/branding, are a sonification layered on top — made up, plainly, same as this project's other demos. Captions are a genuine live decode (a cross-validated readout calibrated on the real contexts), not an echo of whichever context is driving a fly, so they can legitimately read low-confidence or as the "wrong" context.
