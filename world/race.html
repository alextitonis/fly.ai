        How a race works

        • Five legs. Each leg every fly smells the fruit; how strong the smell is changes lane to lane and leg to leg.
        • Real brains. Each fly is its own copy of the fruit-fly connectome with its own neural noise. Its walking neurons' spikes set how far it moves.
        • No steering. No game rule moves a fly; the finish order is whatever the brains did.

        Before your first real race

        • You race with real $FLYAI from your balance. You can lose all of it.
        • The server commits to its seed before each race and reveals it after, so you can check every race.
        • Winnings go to your balance. Withdrawals are sent by hand and can take a day.
        • Betting may be illegal where you live. It's your responsibility to follow your local laws.
