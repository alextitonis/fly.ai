    How a fight works

    • Stats from traits, nothing hidden. Every Trader Fly gets Power, Guard, Vitality and Fury from its traits by the tables below.
    • Ten rounds. Each round both flies' real brains smell the rival and see it charge. We count charge spikes and escape spikes.
    • Damage. hit = charge x 2 x (1 + 0.25% x Power) x (1 + 0.5% x Fury while under half HP). block = escape x 0.8 x (1 + 0.6% x Guard). Damage = hit minus the rival's block. HP = 200 + Vitality.
    • Single elimination. Fights end on a knockout or after ten rounds on points. The two beaten semi-finalists fight for 3rd.
    • Provably fair. The server commits to its seed when registration opens, every entry brings its own seed, and the seed is revealed at the end: Verify replays a fight on your browser.
    • The pot. Every entry and potion goes in. The house fee comes out; the rest is 60% / 25% / 15% for the top three. Too few flies: everything is refunded.
