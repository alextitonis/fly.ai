Your bets

    How betting works
    • Free play stays free. "Spin the drum" never touches money; betting is only this panel.
    • You against the house. Every seat wins 1 time in n. A win pays stake × n × 0.95; the 5% is the house edge.
    • Your balance is the same $FLYAI balance compute orders use: deposits, winnings and order refunds all land in it, and any of it can be bet, spent on orders or withdrawn.
    • Separate from the miners' pool. Bets don't earn compute points, don't change anyone's share of the monthly pool, and no stakes or house edge go into it. The house edge stays with fly.ai.
    • Provably fair. The server commits to its seed before your bet and reveals it after; "Verify this game" replays it on your own fly brains.

    Table talk

      Before your first bet

      • You bet real $FLYAI from your balance. You can lose all of it.
      • Each fly at the table is equally likely to win. A win pays your stake times the number of flies, less a 5% house edge.
      • The server plays the game: it commits to its seed before your bet and reveals it after, so you can check every game.
      • Winnings go to your balance. Withdrawals are sent by hand and can take a day.
      • Betting may be illegal where you live. It's your responsibility to follow your local laws.
