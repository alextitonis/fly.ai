How it works

The pot. Half of the Fly Desk fees (from 22 October) and half of the $FLYAI creator fees from Pons (from 18 October) go in. Each $70 is one copy.

Who's in. Every wallet with at least 100,000 $FLYAI, held or staked. Team wallets, Fly Wallets and contracts are left out.

Odds. Each $FLYAI is one ticket: 500,000 $FLYAI has five times the chance of 100,000.

Fair draw. When the bar fills, the list of holders is fixed first. A block mined a few minutes later picks the winner, so nobody can know it in advance. The list and the block are shown with every draw.

Winning. Sign in with the winning wallet and leave an email, Discord or X handle so we can send your copy. Wallets that farm or split holdings can be skipped, and the copy is drawn again.
