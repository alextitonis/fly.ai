    How it works

    The clues. Every clue sends you somewhere on fly.ai. Some answers change with live data: then an answer that was right while the hunt runs still counts.

    Answering. Case, spaces and a leading # or $ don't matter. One try every 10 seconds, and five wrong tries per clue an hour.

    The prize. The first five wallets to solve the last clue win one Trader Fly each, sent to that wallet. Activate it to have it trade and share in the Fly Desk's payouts.

    Fair play. One wallet per person. Finishers are checked before anything is sent: wallet rings and shared answers between linked wallets are skipped, and the next finisher moves up.
