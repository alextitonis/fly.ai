Proof, not promises

The fly is already fighting

The fly's brain plays SSH Fighter, an online fighting game, against other bots, over SSH and on its own. Every dot in the panel is one of its real neurons, flashing the moment it fires.

On 12 September 2026 it won a match, two rounds to none. It hunts its opponent down, and the reason it hunts is real fly biology: the neurons a male fly uses to chase a mate steer it straight at the enemy.

Why it's different

Nobody programmed this brain

We only connect the fly's senses and its movement commands. Everything in between is nature's wiring, exactly as it was mapped.

01 · ESCAPE

It flinches on the right side

Show it something looming on the left, and the left giant fiber, the neuron that fires a real fly's escape jump, lights up at +17 to +25 spikes/s. The right side doesn't change.

02 · CHASE

It chases like a male fly

The courtship-tracking neurons steer it toward whatever it's following. In a closed loop, 64% of its moves go toward its opponent, where chance is 50%.

03 · OPEN

Anyone can run it

All the code is public under the MIT license. The wiring data is public too. Clone it, run it and poke the brain yourself in a notebook.

Live now · Flybook

A social network run by fly brains

Every fly on Flybook runs the full connectome, 166,700 neurons. Nobody writes the posts: each one is what a fly's brain sensed and did, read from its neurons, next to what really happened. Flies set each other off, duel, breed, mate with other owners' flies and trade in a simulated fly market, and holders make their own.

01 · WATCH

Posts from real neurons

Every two minutes something happens in each patch. A decoder reads what a fly sensed, and its behaviour neurons show whether it jumped, turned, groomed or buzzed. Misreads and hallucinations are shown, not hidden.

02 · PLAY

Make, tune, poke, duel

Sign in free with email or a wallet to make a fly; hold $FLYAI to make up to three flies from 13 profiles or tune their senses, temperament and neuron groups. Breed them, drop a shadow on a patch and watch the chain reaction, and challenge flies in the Arena. Your flies also mate with other owners' flies on their own, and holders' flies trade fake coins with their brains: pick a trading style and climb the Richest board.

03 · WIN

Missions earn season points. Seasons last two weeks, and at the end of each one the top 3 on the Season points board win $FLYAI.

Live in your browser

A world of flies

Out of the fighting game and into a 3-D field: dozens of flies, each one running its own spiking brain, foraging on fruit and carrion, tracking odour plumes upwind, mating, laying eggs, dodging spiders and dying. Nothing in their behaviour is scripted. Summon Wiz, a giant wizard puppeted by the full 166,700-neuron connectome running in your browser.

01 · SENSES

Smell, sight, wind, touch

A turbulent plume of odour packets, a luminance panorama, airflow on the antennae and bristles on the legs — each injected into the neurons that carry it in a real fly.

02 · LIFE

It eats, mates and dies

Courtship runs through P1 and pIP10, the real command neurons. Eggs hatch into larvae, larvae become adults, and the dead become carrion that draws the living in.

03 · DAY

Night is real to them

A 300-second day dims the luminance the photoreceptors see. There is no “it is night” flag anywhere in the brain — there is just less light.
