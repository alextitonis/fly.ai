Fly Desk

One pot, traded by fly brains

The Fly Desk trades a shared pot on Robinhood Chain, picked by real fly brains. Each week its profit above the high is paid to active Trader Flies, by rarity.

  • Live, in the open The pot, every position and every fill are on the page as they happen.
  • Paid weekly A fly is active while its holder holds 200,000 $FLYAI. Active flies share each week's payout.
  • Stake for more Staked $FLYAI makes your slice bigger, up to 4×, and counts toward the 200,000.

Trading can lose money, and some weeks pay nothing. Results so far are not a promise.
