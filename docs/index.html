Flies that trade real money

A Trader Fly is an NFT with a wallet of its own. A real fly brain picks its trades, the Fly Desk trades a shared pot, and holders are paid from it every week.

  1. Get a flyBuy one on the market, or breed two into one.
  2. Fund its walletIts brain trades from it around the clock. You can withdraw any time.
  3. Watch it tradeEvery trade and result is public on the leaderboard.
  4. ClaimCollect your flies' share of the desk's payout in $FLYAI.

Trading can lose money, and some weeks pay nothing. Results so far are not a promise.

Why it's different

Nobody programmed this brain

We only connect the fly's senses and its movement commands. Everything in between is nature's wiring, exactly as it was mapped.
