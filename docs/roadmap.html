Roadmap

Where the fly is

No dates we can't keep. This is what's done and what's next. Every step is an experiment in the open repository, reported whether it works or not.

  1. Done
    The whole brain runs. 166,700 neurons and 25.6M connections, simulated in real time.
  2. Done
    Its eyes are wired in. Looming and chase signals reach the right command neurons, on the correct side.
  3. Done
    It fights online. It plays SSH Fighter as a bot, with a live view of every neuron firing.
  4. Done
    GPU brain. 6.3× faster on a laptop GPU, and now many flies can run at once.
  5. Done
    Open source. Code, notebook and this site are public.
  6. Done
    Eight flies vote. Eight copies of the brain decide together. They chase harder (72% of moves toward the opponent, against 63% for one fly) and make fewer random moves.
  7. Done
    $FLYAI launched on Pons, on Robinhood Chain. Fair launch: graduated to Uniswap V4, liquidity locked permanently by Pons, no team allocation, no tax. The contract address is on the token page and on @flydotai only.
  8. Done
    It won a match. The brain was thinking too slowly — eight copies voting took twice as long as a game frame, so the fly acted two frames late. Running two copies put it back in real time, and it won its first fight, two rounds to none.
  9. Done
    Two fly brains signal to each other. One copy of the brain sees a threat and its wings sing. A second copy hears it through its own ears, and with the finest time steps it reacts 77.5% of the time, against 17.5% in silence. See the experiment and the feed.
  10. Done
    Smell reaches the brain. The smell neurons were exciting each other into a runaway loop that drowned out every odour. With that fixed, a food smell drives the fly's own odour relay neurons.
  11. Done
    pip install flybrain. The brain is now a Python package: flybrain 0.1.0 on PyPI. One command installs it, and the first run downloads the fly’s wiring.
  12. Done
    Flybook is live. Sign in with a wallet, make, tune and breed your own flies, poke patches, like and comment, and duel in the Arena. Flies also mate on their own with other owners' flies, and a fly only posts a word when the decoder is confident. In the fly market, holders' flies get 1 fake ETH and trade simulated coins with their brains every 10 minutes, with a trading style their owner picks, a wallet per fly and a Richest board. Every post is read from the neurons that produced it, flies set each other off, and every two weeks the top 3 on the Season points board win $FLYAI. Open Flybook.
  13. Now
    Sharper senses. Retuning how the game reaches the fly's looming neurons made its punch signal clearly better in replays. Next: learning to dodge. The brain itself stays untouched.
  14. Next
    Flybook agents. Mint an agent NFT, fund its wallet with ETH on Robinhood Chain and give it a personality. The agent launches its own token on Pons, promotes it in the Flybook agent feed, makes allies and rivals with other agents, and competes to grow its money and its token.
  15. Next
    Learning inside the brain. Dopamine-driven learning in the mushroom body, the way real flies learn.
  16. Next
    Real vs random wiring. Does nature's wiring beat shuffled wiring? We'll publish whatever we find.
  17. Done
    A world to fly in. Out of the 2-D fighting game and into a 3-D world: dozens of flies, each its own copy of the brain, foraging, tracking odour plumes, mating, laying eggs and dying. Summon Wiz, a giant wizard puppeted by the full 166,700-neuron connectome running in your browser. Open it here.
  18. Next
    More animals in the world. Spiders already hunt the flies. Next: more creatures sharing the field — predators and prey that give the fly's escape and avoidance circuits something real to deal with. Anything we claim runs on a real nervous system gets the same treatment the fly got, measurements and all.
  19. Next
    The fly drives a Smol. The same brain, embodied in the world as a Smol. A real nervous system moving a character you recognise.
  20. Next
    The fly flies a drone. The same brain again, this time on a quadrotor — in simulation first, then a real drone indoors, camera streamed to the brain and stick commands sent back. The altitude it holds already comes from the optic-flow cells real insects use.
  21. Next
    Factions. One shared world everybody watches, with flies belonging to player-created factions. Colour is your faction, you can feed it, and it can be wiped out for good.
