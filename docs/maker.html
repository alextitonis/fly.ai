Fly Maker

Market making for your token

Tell it what your token needs, fund a vault your own wallet owns, and fly.ai's bot keeps the market liquid: a steady band of liquidity around the price, or a slow buyback or sale. Every trade stays inside guardrails the vault checks on chain, and you can pause or withdraw any time.

Fly Maker isn't live yet: you can look up pools and build plans, but vaults can't be created until the contracts are deployed.

Good to know

Your vault is yours. It is created and owned by your wallet. Money can only go back to you, into the vault's one GIGA DEX pool as part of a trade, or to the deposit fee. The bot can trade and move liquidity in that pool; it can't withdraw, change your plan or your guardrails, or unpause.

The plan is public. The vault stores the hash of the exact plan text you signed and publishes the text on chain. This page recomputes the hash in your browser; on a mismatch, don't sign.

Guardrails. Max trade, a daily cap (any 24 hours, no midnight double), max price impact per trade, and a price check against the pool's 5-minute average, so a pump is never chased. Liquidity moves have their own daily budget.

It earns swap fees. With a steady band, your vault is a liquidity provider on GIGA DEX and collects the swap fees its liquidity earns back into the vault. On a standard GIGA pool LPs keep 90% of those fees and 10% goes to GIGA DEX (veGIGA stakers). Gauged and creator pools pay LPs no swap fees; each pool's share is shown when you pick it.

New tokens. No GIGA pool yet? Create one here at a starting price you choose; the bot seeds it from your vault with a band beside that price.

Risks. Prices move, liquidity can hold more of whichever asset is falling (impermanent loss), every trade pays the pool fee, and the bot can misjudge a market. Small pools mean small trades. Only use what you can afford to lose.
