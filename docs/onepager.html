What it is

A real brain, not a chatbot

The trading decisions come from a full fruit-fly connectome: 166,700 neurons and 25.6 million connections, mapped by FlyEM at HHMI Janelia. The code is open source (MIT).

Trader Flies

An NFT with its own wallet. The owner deposits, picks a style (steady baskets, stock tokens, or high-risk sniping) and the fly trades on Robinhood Chain, Base, BNB, Arbitrum and Solana.

The Fly Desk

The house trading desk. Each week it pays fly holders a share of its results, in $FLYAI bought on the market.

On-chain agents

Every active fly is registered as an ERC-8004 agent with its own on-chain identity, and owners rate them on-chain. Good flies stand out; others can follow them.

$FLYAI

The token on Robinhood Chain, launched on Pons. What uses it:

  • Activating a fly needs 200,000 $FLYAI held (in a wallet, staked or locked); that unlocks every feature.
  • Every withdrawal is paid in $FLYAI. A fly's positions are sold and the proceeds buy $FLYAI on the market.
  • Desk payouts are bought on the market every week: 3.1M $FLYAI so far.
  • Burns: fly chat packs and part of every follow pass are burned.
  • Fee drop (a 2-month test): the month's Pons trading fees go to holders, split by average holding.
