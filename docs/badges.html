    How it works

    Earning. Your clock starts when your wallet reaches the amount and restarts if it drops below. Staked and locked $FLYAI counts as held.

    Claiming. Claim any badges you've earned in one transaction. You only pay the gas, on Robinhood Chain.

    Keeping. A claimed badge stays even if you sell later. Badges can only be removed for a mistake or abuse.
