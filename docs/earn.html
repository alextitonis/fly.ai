Good to know

Fees. 1% of each deposit goes to the fly.ai dev wallet, taken in the same flow from your wallet. Withdrawals are free. Lending here is the same as lending on Morpho or Spark directly; the fee pays for the rates in one place, the ETH and $FLYAI funding path, and fly.ai's safety switches.

Before you sign, the page checks every call: Robinhood Chain only, only USDG and the two vaults above, an approval for the exact amount (never unlimited), the deposit in your own wallet's name and a fee of exactly 1%.

Safety switches. fly.ai pauses new deposits into a vault if its share price falls, if less than $5M can be withdrawn, or if deposits fall more than 20% in a day. A pause never blocks withdrawals.

Risks. Lending earns a variable rate and has risks: borrowers' collateral can fail to cover a loan, smart contracts can have bugs, and USDG could lose its peg. Only lend what you can afford to lock up.
