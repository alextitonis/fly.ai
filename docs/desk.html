    How staking works

    Stake $FLYAI to get a bigger slice of the weekly payout: 1M staked = 1.5×, 2M = 2×, 5M = 3×, 10M or more = 4× (the most). It counts right away, on what you have staked now. The pot stays the same size, so it's your slice next to holders who stake less. Staked $FLYAI also counts toward the 200,000 that keeps your flies activated. Unstaking takes 7 days.

    Payouts aren't switched on yet: these are the amounts each fly would get. Once they are, a week's payout settles on Sunday at 23:00 UTC and can be claimed on the Claim page two days later.

      Holder chat

      Holders talk, the desk listens. Name a token ($SYMBOL or its address) and the desk runs its own rug checks on it right here. Each message carries its writer's weight: bigger bags held longer count more. Tokens with enough weight behind them that pass the checks join the flies' view. Nothing is ever bought just because the chat said so.

      Holder vote

      $FLYAI holders pick how the flies trade tomorrow. One vote per wallet per day, weighted by the dollars of $FLYAI it holds when the day closes. Biggest bag wins. Signing is free, no gas.

      How it trades

      The brain decides. Each token the fly sees becomes a smell on its olfactory receptor neurons; its mushroom body learns from dopamine what paid. What its motor neurons do in that bar is turned into buy, hold or sell.

      Real prices, real routes. Robinhood stock tokens, Pons memes and Virtuals agent tokens, each traded through the Uniswap route it would really take, with pool fees, price impact and token taxes charged.

      Paper, shadow or live. The pill at the top says which: on paper nothing is sent; in shadow every trade is quoted on the chain but not sent; live, the desk trades from its own wallet, a slice at a time.
