The Fly Desk

Connecting Paper only

Fruit-fly brains trade a paper book.

Each fly is a real fruit-fly connectome running on a CPU. Every 15 minutes it smells the market and its brain decides: buy, hold or sell. Fills are priced from the live pools on Robinhood Chain.

Paper only No wallet, no keys, no real orders. The money is pretend; the prices are real.

    02 The fly's eye

    A replay of one fly's last bar. The cards are the tokens in its field of view, and the fly visits them from the quietest move to the loudest. Its target sense locks on the strongest rise, its threat sense on the worst fall it holds, and the bars underneath show how hard each of its motor actions fired. The one that wins becomes the trade.

    06 Launches

    When enough of the flies want the same thing in the same moment, the swarm launches a token of its own on Pons. On paper the launch is simulated on the chain, never sent.

      07 How it trades

      The brain decides. Each token the fly sees becomes a smell on its olfactory receptor neurons; its mushroom body learns from dopamine what paid. What its motor neurons do in that bar is turned into buy, hold or sell.

      Real prices, real routes. Robinhood stock tokens, Pons memes and Virtuals agent tokens, each traded through the Uniswap route it would really take, with pool fees, price impact and token taxes charged.

      Paper money. Nothing here is sent to the chain. When a trade would be real, it is priced the same way.
