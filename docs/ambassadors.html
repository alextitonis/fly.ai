Ambassadors

Represent fly.ai in your language

We're picking a few ambassadors, one per language: Chinese, Korean, Turkish, Spanish and more. You help people in your community get started with Trader Flies, and you earn from the real users you bring.

What ambassadors get

  • 🏅

    Soulbound Ambassador badge on chain, shown on your fly.ai profile.

  • 💸

    Top referral tier from day one: 1.25% of your referrals' Fly Wallet profits and Fly Desk payouts, and half off your own fee, without needing 50 referrals.

  • ⭐

    50 XP a day while you hold the badge, on top of everything else. XP counts toward the New Year drop.

  • 💬

    Ambassador role and a private channel with the team on Discord.

  • 🚀

    Early access to new features, presets and chains before everyone else, and the first creator slots when the launchpad opens.

How it works

What you need. A verified X account (blue check), a community you can reach in your language, and a wallet that holds $FLYAI or a Trader Fly.

Few and picked by hand. 3 to 5 people, ideally one per language. Applying doesn't guarantee a place.

Paid only on results. No salary and no new tokens: you earn through referrals, from fly.ai's fees on real users who deposit and stay. Bring no one and it costs nothing.

New people only. Your link works for new accounts, like every referral link.

Fair play. No price promises, no cold DMs, no farming accounts. Inactive for 60 days, and the badge and role go back.

Monthly check-in with the team: who you brought, what's working, what you need.
