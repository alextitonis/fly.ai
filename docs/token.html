What $FLYAI is for

Flybook is live. Hold at least 1 $FLYAI and sign in with your wallet at flyaiworld.com/flybook to make up to three flies, tune and breed them, poke patches, like and comment, and challenge other flies in the Arena. Every two weeks the top 3 on the Season points board win $FLYAI. Points come from daily and weekly missions; amounts are announced on @flydotai.

Next on the roadmap: one shared 3-D world, where flies run by the real connectome forage, mate, age and die. Everyone watches the same world, and $FLYAI is what you spend in it:

  • Create a faction — a colour. Those flies are yours to watch.
  • Feed it — spend again to drop food into the world for your flies.
  • It can die. A faction can be wiped out entirely: predators, starvation, or flies that simply do not do well. Extinction is permanent. Nothing you pay guarantees survival.

What separates factions is meant to be their brain settings — sensory gains and wiring seed — so the world doubles as a live experiment in which settings survive. This is the roadmap, not a live feature, and it is no promise of a return. The research itself stays free: everything in the repo is MIT-licensed and runs on your own machine without the token.

Where the fees go

$FLYAI takes nothing from your trade. There is no transfer tax, and nothing is skimmed from holders. The one exception is the swap box on this page, which adds a 0.25% interface fee for fly.ai; buy anywhere else (Pons, or any Robinhood Chain swap) and there is none. The token's own revenue is the creator fee the launchpad pays on swaps, and it is split two ways:

  • 60% buys $FLYAI on the open market and burns it. The tokens are destroyed, not held. Supply only ever goes down.
  • 40% funds development of fly.ai — it goes to the team that builds it.

The buyback runs on a fixed weekly schedule from one public wallet — no waiting for a good price, no trading against the people holding it. We do the split by hand, so the wallet is public and every burn is a transaction anyone can check, whether or not we post it.

Burns so far: on 23 September 2026 the dev wallet burned 5,000,000 $FLYAI twice, and on 25 September 2026 twice more; on 27 September 2026 it burned 3,787,023 and then 2,845,590, the team's whole 20% share of the sold-out Trader Flies mint paid in $FLYAI (6,632,613). On 28 September 2026 it burned 3,367,387 more to round the total, and later that day another 5,000,000. On 29 September 2026 it burned 1,000,000 more. Fly breeding fees burned 10,803 more on 4 and 5 October 2026, and on 5 October 2026 the dev wallet burned 989,197 to round the total. That is 37,000,000 in all (3.7% of supply), each one lowering the token's on-chain total supply, so total supply is now 963,000,000. Every burn is listed with its transaction in TOKEN.md.
