Free desktop app

Fly Pet

A tiny fly that lives on your desktop. It chats with you, tells you what your Trader Fly is doing, shares alpha calls, and coaches you in your games.

Download for Windows macOS: soon Linux: soon

Windows may warn that the app isn't signed yet: click "More info", then "Run anyway". The code is open source on GitHub.

It lives on your desktop

A small fly that walks around near where you put it, stays on top of your windows, sleeps at night and waves good morning. Grab it, toss it, feed it.

Chat with your fly

Click the fly and talk to your Trader Fly or partner NFT: ask why it traded, tell it what to change. It only suggests: nothing changes until you approve it.

Trades and price alerts

It reacts to its own buys and sells, gains and losses, and big $FLYAI moves, with a bubble and a notification if you want one.

Alpha calls

The same calls as our Discord, right in its chat, with Watch, Chart and Copy buttons.

Game coach

Playing a game? Your fly finds it by itself and, when a pick, shop or level-up opens, tells you the best choice with its odds. Free hints every day; Coach mode watches all session with the Coach pass.

Safe by design

You sign in with your wallet on flyaiworld.com: the app gets a pet key that can chat and change your fly's settings, never move money, and never asks for a seed phrase. The coach only looks at the one game window, and never touches your game.

Game coach

It suggests. You make every move.

Get started

  1. Download and open Fly Pet. The fly appears at the bottom of your screen.
  2. Click Connect and approve in your browser on flyaiworld.com.
  3. Pick which of your flies (or partner NFTs) lives on your desktop.
  4. Click the fly to chat. Playing a game? Tap "Coach mode" in its chat, or press Alt+Shift+C for a hint.

To close it: right-click the fly, or use its icon in the system tray.
