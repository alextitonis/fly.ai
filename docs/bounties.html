Rules

  • One X account per wallet, on every bounty. You're paid to the wallet you signed in with.
  • Your own work only. No bots, no AI-written spam, no farming with many wallets. We check winners' wallets on-chain before paying, and farmers are blocked.
  • You can edit your entry until we review it. Entries stay private until they win.
  • The team picks the winners. A bounty can end with fewer winners than planned if the entries aren't good enough.
  • Found a security bug? Don't post it publicly. Send a DM to @flydotai first, then enter the security bounty with a private link.
