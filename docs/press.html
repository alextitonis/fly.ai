Describe fly.ai

In one line

fly.ai runs the complete brain of a real fruit fly, 166,700 neurons from the MaleCNS connectome, as open-source software, and lets it trade on Robinhood Chain.

In one paragraph

fly.ai simulates the whole central nervous system of an adult male fruit fly from the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome: 166,700 neurons and 25.6 million connections, open source under the MIT license. The same brain drives Trader Flies, NFTs that each trade their own on-chain wallet, and the Fly Desk, one shared pot traded by fly brains that pays active Trader Flies every week. $FLYAI, its token on Robinhood Chain, funds the work.
