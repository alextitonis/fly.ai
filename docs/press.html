About fly.ai

One-line and one-paragraph descriptions built from verified project facts, ready to copy.

One-line description

fly.ai runs the complete 166,700-neuron brain connectome of an adult fruit fly open source on your computer and live on Robinhood Chain, powering Trader Flies, the Fly Desk, and the $FLYAI token.

One-paragraph description

fly.ai runs the complete brain wiring of an adult male fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster — 166,700 neurons and 25.6 million synapses from the Janelia MaleCNS v1.0 connectome) as an open-source spiking neural simulation. With no language model or hand-written strategy inside the brain, sensory inputs and motor neurons drive real-time applications: Trader Flies (1,000 genesis NFTs on Robinhood Chain with their own trading wallets), the Fly Desk (a shared pot traded by fruit-fly brains), a 3-D biological world simulation, Flybook, and distributed browser compute. The project is funded by the $FLYAI token on Robinhood Chain.
