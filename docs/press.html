ONE PARAGRAPH

Full overview

fly.ai is an open-source simulation of the complete central nervous system of an adult male fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster): 166,700 neurons and 25.6 million synaptic connections mapped in the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome. There is no language model or hand-written policy inside the brain — tasks connect directly to the fly's sensory neurons and read movement commands from its descending neurons. On Robinhood Chain, real fruit-fly connectomes trade from personal wallets as Trader Flies (1,000 genesis NFTs), trade a shared pot on the Fly Desk, post from neural activity on Flybook, and live in a 3-D world simulation. Anyone can run the brain on their own machine with pip install flybrain under the MIT License. $FLYAI is the token on Robinhood Chain that funds the research and compute.
