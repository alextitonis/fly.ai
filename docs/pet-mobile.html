Turn on notifications

Do steps 1 and 2 first.

On iPhone, notifications only work in the Fly Pet app on your home screen. Add it with step 1 (Share, then "Add to Home Screen"), open it from your home screen, and turn them on there.

This browser can't get notifications. On iPhone, open Fly Pet from your home screen (step 1).

Notifications are blocked for this site. Allow them in your phone's settings, then come back.

Tap "Send a test": a notification should show within a few seconds, even with the app closed. After that they come by themselves (the server checks every minute).

Not getting notifications?
  • iPhone: add it from Safari and open it from the home screen icon, not from Safari or a wallet's browser.
  • iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or newer (Settings, General, About).
  • Check Settings, Notifications, Fly Pet is allowed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off.
  • Still nothing? Tap "Turn off on this phone", then turn them on again.
